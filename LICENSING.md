# a.n.c.r Pro licences

**Buy, renew and manage your licences**

a.n.c.r is free for any use, and everything in it stays free. **a.n.c.r Pro**
adds what runs automatically and repeatedly: data-driven runs, load tests and
MCP server checks, with HTML reports and baselines. A Pro licence turns them
on. This page explains how to get one, renew it and buy more.

To buy or renew, email **[ashokkumar.ta@gmail.com](mailto:ashokkumar.ta@gmail.com)** with the
details below. You'll get your licence by email as a `.ancr-licence` file.

---

## Choosing a licence

**Who it's for.** A licence is one of three kinds:

| Kind | Works on | What to send when you buy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organisation** | Any of your organisation's computers, for the number of people (seats) you buy | The organisation's name, a contact email and the number of seats |
| **One person** | Any computer that person uses; activated with their email address | The person's name and email address |
| **One computer** | Only the computer it's issued for | The computer's **machine ID** (see below), and a name and email for the licence |

**How long it lasts.**

- **Perpetual:** works for good with every version released until its
  updates end. A version released after that asks for a renewed licence;
  the versions you already have keep working with it.
- **Subscription:** works with every version until its end date, then Pro
  turns off until it's renewed.
- **Trial:** a short subscription, to try Pro before you buy.

**What it includes.** A licence can include all of Pro or some of it (for
example, only load tests). Say which features you need, or ask for all of
them.

## Buying a licence

1. Decide the kind of licence, its term and the features you need.
2. For a licence for one computer, open ancr on that computer, go to
   **⚙ → Licence…** and click **Copy** next to the machine ID.
3. Email **ashokkumar.ta@gmail.com** with the kind, the term, the features, and
   the details for that kind of licence (in the table above).
4. You'll get a quote and, once it's paid, the licence file.

## Adding your licence

1. In ancr, open **⚙ → Licence…**.
2. Paste the licence into the box and click **Add licence**, or click
   **Choose licence file…** and pick the `.ancr-licence` file.
3. For a licence for one person, enter the email address it was issued to,
   then click **Activate**.

The panel then shows who the licence is for, its term, what it includes and
when its updates end. The licence is checked on your computer: nothing is
sent anywhere, and there's no account.

## Renewing a licence

**⚙ → Licence…** shows when your licence's updates end and, for a
subscription or trial, when it ends. To renew, click **Renew or buy more**
there, or email **ashokkumar.ta@gmail.com** with your licence ID (shown in
the panel as **Licence**) before that date. You'll get a renewed licence
file: add it as above, and it takes the old one's place.

- **Perpetual:** renewing extends its updates, so newer versions run Pro too.
  Without renewing, keep the version you have: Pro keeps working in it.
- **Subscription or trial:** renewing moves its end date. After the end
  date, Pro turns off until a renewed licence is added. Your workspaces,
  requests and reports aren't affected, and everything free keeps working.

## Buying more licences

To add seats to an organisation's licence, or to license more people or
computers, click **Renew or buy more** in **⚙ → Licence…**, or email
**ashokkumar.ta@gmail.com** with your licence ID and what you need. An organisation's
licence is reissued with the new number of seats.

## Moving to another computer

- **Organisation** and **one person** licences: add the same licence on the
  new computer, and remove it from the old one (**⚙ → Licence… → Remove
  licence**).
- **One computer** licences: send the new computer's machine ID with your
  licence ID, and you'll get a licence for it. The machine ID also changes
  when the operating system is reinstalled; send the new one in the same way.

## If a licence isn't accepted

**⚙ → Licence…** says why: for example, it's for another product, it has
ended, this version is newer than its updates, it's for another computer, or
it has been withdrawn. Email **ashokkumar.ta@gmail.com** with your licence ID and what the
panel says.
